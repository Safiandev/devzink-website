01 / The problem

Most products ship AI the hard way.

3–6 monthsThe wrong build timeline

Many builds take 3–6 months to reach an MVP. By the time it's live, your market has moved.

2×The rewrite tax

Products not architected for AI get rewritten after product-market fit — often at a multiple of the original cost.

Bolt-onWhy AI features break

Not because the model is bad — because they were bolted on. No vector layer, prompts hardcoded in the frontend, and they break under load.

Every product we ship is AI-native from the first commit. No rewrites. No bolt-ons. No surprises at scale.

03 / Two build paths

What actually changes when AI is in the architecture.

Bolted on later

Six months after launch

  • Prompts hardcoded in the frontend
  • No vector layer — every AI feature is a fresh API call
  • API keys exposed in the browser
  • Rate limits hit the moment traffic grows
  • Months lost to a rewrite
  • A competitor shipped it first

AI-native from day one

Six months after launch

  • Prompts versioned, tested, and server-side
  • Vector DB handles retrieval + semantic search
  • Keys secured behind a proper AI gateway
  • Built to scale without re-architecture
  • New AI features ship in days, not quarters
  • Roadmap keeps moving — no rewrite in sight

Same idea. Same six months. The difference is whether you build for AI first, or retrofit it later.
