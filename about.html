What we are. What we're not.

We are

  • Engineers and designers who write the code themselves
  • A small team that takes 2–4 clients at a time
  • AI-first in everything we build — it's our core skill
  • Obsessed with shipping — weekly demos, not status reports
  • Transparent about scope, pricing, and timelines from day one
  • Available to clients directly, not through account managers

We're not

  • An agency that outsources your project to juniors
  • A "full-service" shop that says yes to everything
  • The cheapest option — we compete on quality
  • Fast-talkers who promise everything and deliver half
  • A vendor who disappears after the invoice clears
  • The right fit for every project — we'll tell you if we're not
