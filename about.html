How we work

Four principles we don't compromise on.

Ship fast, ship real

The first working demo lands in your hands within weeks — not months. You see progress, not slide decks.

Your IP, your code

100% ownership transferred from day one. Clean repos, documented handover, no lock-in. Ever.

Honest over easy

If AI isn't the right answer for your problem, we'll tell you. If we're not the right team for the job, we'll say so.

Fewer clients, deeper work

We take 2–4 active clients at a time. Every one gets senior attention — no junior handoff, no queue behind bigger accounts.

SMBs & mid-market

Automating operations

Teams drowning in manual work who need AI agents and workflows to scale without adding headcount. Usually a 4–8 week engagement, then ongoing support.

Founders & startups

Building AI products
