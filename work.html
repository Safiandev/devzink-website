AI Agent · B2B Sales

01 / 09

Multi-Lead Qualification & Auto-Email Agent

The problem

Leads arrive from forms and webhooks all day. Sorting them by hand is slow, hot leads wait hours for a reply, and low-budget enquiries eat the team's time.

What we built

An agent that scores every incoming lead with Gemini as hot, warm or cold, logs it to the right sheet, alerts the sales rep, and sends the lead a tailored email. Qualified form leads also get a ready-to-send meeting email drafted in Gmail.

  • Hot / warm / cold scoring
  • Instant tailored emails
  • Gmail meeting drafts
  • n8n
  • Google Gemini
  • Structured output
  • Google Sheets

AI Agent · Home Services

03 / 09

Home Services Missed-Call Agent

The problem

Home service owners are on job sites and miss calls. Callers move on to the next plumber or HVAC company, bookings happen by phone tag, and reminders and review requests are forgotten.

What we built

Four workflows working as one system: missed calls get a text-back and are booked over SMS with calendar clash checks, customers get 24h and 2h reminders, finished jobs trigger review requests, and the owner gets a daily digest plus instant error alerts.

  • Missed call → SMS booking
  • 24h + 2h reminders
  • Auto review requests
  • n8n
  • Twilio SMS
  • Google Calendar
  • Google Sheets
  • Gmail
